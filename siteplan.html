Website Project Plan

Restaurant 30 de Junho

Site Name: Adventure Trails

Reason: The website is for the restaurant "30 de Junho," aimed at better connecting with customers by simplifying table reservations, special requests, and communication between customers and the business. The site includes three pages – Home, Events, and Contact. It features the location, a map, communication channels, and upcoming events.

Site Purpose

Currently, the restaurant does not have a page to help customers easily find us. Additionally, the website will increase the restaurant's visibility online, making it easier for potential customers to discover us. It will also provide a platform for collecting direct feedback from customers, helping us improve our services and better meet their needs.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find the restaurant?
  • Do they have events there?
